Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Follower Insight application for mobile devices (the "Application").

The Application is developed and operated by Naomi.Jpn Japan, an independent software project based in Japan ("Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and have designed the Application to process your Instagram export data locally on your device.

1. Instagram Data and Local Processing

The Application allows you to select and analyse Instagram data export files stored on your device.

Depending on the feature used, these files may contain information such as:

  • Instagram usernames
  • Follower information
  • Following information
  • Dates or timestamps associated with follower or following relationships

The Application processes this information locally on your device.

Your imported Instagram files and the information contained in them are not uploaded or transmitted to the Service Provider.

The Service Provider does not have access to your Instagram account, Instagram password, or imported Instagram data.

The Application does not require you to log in to Instagram and does not connect directly to your Instagram account.

Optional saved History. If you choose to save a comparison to History, the Application stores summary numbers and dates on your device only: the dates of the two exports, follower totals, and the counts of followers who stayed, are new, or stopped following. If you track more than one Instagram account, you may optionally type a short account label (for example "Personal" or "Business") to keep each account's saved history separate; this label is text you write yourself and is stored on your device only. Saved History does not contain usernames. Nothing is saved to History unless you tap "Save snapshot". See Section 6 for how to remove it.

Optional permitted accounts list. If you mark accounts as "permitted" in the Follow-for-Follow screen (for example brands or public figures you do not expect to follow you back), the Application stores those usernames on your device only, so they can be hidden in future analyses. Nothing is added to this list unless you tap "Permit". See Section 6 for how to remove it.

Optional re-export reminder. The History tab includes an optional switch that, once turned on, uses your device's local notification system to remind you, on a fixed monthly schedule, to re-export your Instagram data and run a new comparison. This reminder is off by default. Turning it on may prompt Android to ask for notification permission; the Application does not request this permission unless you turn the reminder on. The reminder is entirely local: no information about you, your device, or your use of the Application is transmitted to the Service Provider or any third party as part of this feature, and it does not use any remote push-notification service. You can turn the reminder off at any time from the History tab.

First-time guidance tours. The Application may show a short, one-time guided tour highlighting features on the Follower Tracker, Follow-for-Follow, and History screens. Whether you have already seen each tour is stored on your device only, so it is not shown again; this setting contains no personal or Instagram data and is never sent to us.

Disclaimer acceptance. When you accept the disclaimer shown on first launch, the Application stores on your device that you have accepted it (and which version of the disclaimer you accepted), so it is not shown again every time you open the Application. If the disclaimer changes materially, you will be asked to accept the new version once. This setting contains no personal or Instagram data and is never sent to us.

2. Data Collected by the Service Provider

The Service Provider does not operate a user account system and does not directly collect personal information through the Application.

We do not collect or store:

  • Your Instagram login credentials
  • Your Instagram export files
  • Your follower or following lists
  • Instagram usernames contained in your imported files (including any usernames you mark as permitted, which stay on your device and are never sent to us)
  • The results of analyses performed by the Application (any History you choose to save, including any account label you type, stays on your device and is never sent to us)
  • Whether you have turned the re-export reminder on or off (this setting stays on your device and is never sent to us)
  • Whether you have already seen the Application's first-time guidance tours (this setting stays on your device and is never sent to us)
  • Whether you have accepted the disclaimer (this setting stays on your device and is never sent to us)

We do not sell your personal data.

We do not use imported Instagram data for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

6. Data Retention

Instagram data imported into the Application is processed locally on your device and is not retained by the Service Provider.

Any locally stored information associated with the Application remains subject to your device's storage and application-management functions.

The only information the Application keeps between uses is the optional saved History (including any account label you type), the optional permitted accounts list, your re-export reminder on/off setting, which first-time guidance tours you have already seen, and whether you have accepted the disclaimer, all described in Section 1. Each is stored in the Application's private storage on your device, is not uploaded to the Service Provider, and is excluded from Android cloud backup. They may still be carried over if you use Android's direct device-to-device transfer to move to a new phone.

You can delete individual History entries inside the Application (History tab, delete icon), remove accounts from the permitted list one at a time or all at once (Follow-for-Follow screen, "Remove" or "Clear all"), turn the re-export reminder off (History tab), or remove all locally stored Application data using your device settings (clear storage) or by uninstalling the Application. After clearing storage or reinstalling, the disclaimer is shown again.

Information voluntarily provided through support communications may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided or where retention is required by applicable law.

7. Security

The Application is designed to minimise the transmission of personal data by processing imported Instagram data locally on your device.

No method of electronic processing or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. However, we aim to limit the amount of information accessible to the Service Provider and to avoid unnecessary collection of personal data.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their device and their original Instagram export files.
