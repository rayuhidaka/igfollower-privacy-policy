The Application is developed and operated by Naomi.Jpn Japan, an independent software project based in Japan ("Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and have designed the Application to process your Instagram or Facebook export data locally on your device.

The Application is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram, Facebook or Meta.

1. Instagram and Facebook Data and Local Processing

The Application allows you to select and analyse Instagram or Facebook data export files stored on your device.

Depending on the feature used, these files may contain information such as:

  • Instagram usernames
  • Follower information
  • Following information
  • The names of your Facebook friends, and of people you sent friend requests to
  • Dates or timestamps associated with follower, following or friend relationships and friend requests

The Application processes this information locally on your device.

Your imported export files and the information contained in them are not uploaded or transmitted to the Service Provider.

The Service Provider does not have access to your Instagram or Facebook account, your password, or your imported export data.

The Application does not require you to log in to Instagram or Facebook and does not connect directly to your Instagram or Facebook account.

Profile and search links. Next to each username or name, the Application offers a button that opens Instagram or Facebook in your browser or app: for Instagram the account's profile page, for Facebook a people search for that name (Facebook exports contain no profile links). This happens only when you tap the button. The username or name is then part of the web address sent to Instagram or Facebook, which handle it under their own privacy policies. The Application itself does not send the username or name anywhere.

Optional saved History. If you choose to save a comparison to History, the Application stores summary numbers and dates on your device only: the dates of the two exports, follower or friend totals, the counts of followers or friends who stayed, are new, or were lost, and whether the comparison was of Instagram followers or Facebook friends. If you track more than one account, you may optionally type a short account label (for example "Personal" or "Business") to keep each account's saved history separate; this label is text you write yourself and is stored on your device only. Saved History does not contain usernames or names. Nothing is saved to History unless you tap "Save snapshot". See Section 6 for how to remove it.

Optional permitted accounts list. If you mark accounts as "permitted" in the Follow-for-Follow screen (for example brands or public figures you do not expect to follow you back), the Application stores those usernames on your device only, so they can be hidden in future analyses. Nothing is added to this list unless you tap "Permit". See Section 6 for how to remove it.

Optional re-export reminder. The History tab includes an optional switch that, once turned on, uses your device's local notification system to remind you, on a fixed monthly schedule, to re-export your data and run a new comparison. This reminder is off by default. Turning it on may prompt Android to ask for notification permission; the Application does not request this permission unless you turn the reminder on. The reminder is entirely local: no information about you, your device, or your use of the Application is transmitted to the Service Provider or any third party as part of this feature, and it does not use any remote push-notification service. You can turn the reminder off at any time from the History tab.

First-time guidance tours. The Application may show a short, one-time guided tour highlighting features on the Follower Tracker, Follow-for-Follow, and History screens. Whether you have already seen each tour is stored on your device only, so it is not shown again; this setting contains no personal or imported export data and is never sent to us.

Disclaimer acceptance. When you accept the disclaimer shown on first launch, the Application stores on your device that you have accepted it (and which version of the disclaimer you accepted), so it is not shown again every time you open the Application. If the disclaimer changes materially, you will be asked to accept the new version once. This setting contains no personal or imported export data and is never sent to us.

Ad display history. To show ads no more often than described in Section 4, the Application stores on your device how many analyses you have completed and when it last showed an ad. This contains no personal or imported export data and is never sent to us.

7. Data Handling and Security

This section explains how the personal and sensitive data described above is handled and protected.

Imported export files.

  • The Application opens only the files you pick yourself in Android's system file picker. It does not request permission to read your device's shared storage, photos or other files.
  • To read a selected export, the Application unpacks it into a temporary folder in its private cache directory, which other apps cannot access. Each file in the archive is checked before it is written, so that it can only be placed inside that temporary folder.
  • The temporary folder is deleted as soon as the analysis finishes, whether it succeeds or fails. Your original export file is never changed or deleted.
  • Analysis results, including the usernames and names they contain, are kept only in the device's memory while the Application is open, and are discarded when the Application is closed. They are not written to storage unless you choose one of the options below.

Data stored on your device. The optional information described in Section 1 (History, permitted accounts, reminder, tour, disclaimer and ad display settings) is stored in the Application's private internal storage. Android's app sandbox prevents other apps from reading it, and on devices with storage encryption enabled (the default on modern Android devices) it is encrypted at rest by the operating system. It is excluded from Android cloud backup, as described in Section 6.

Files you export yourself. If you tap "Export CSV" on a result list, or save a recap image, Android's "Save as" screen opens and the file is written only to the location you choose. A CSV file contains the usernames or names in that list; a recap image contains summary numbers and, for Instagram, the username of your longest-standing follower. Once saved, these files are outside the Application and are protected by your device and the storage location you chose.

Data in transit. The Application has no servers of its own and sends no imported export data or analysis results over the internet. The only data transmitted is the advertising information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Section 4), which is sent directly to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Access by the Service Provider. The Service Provider has no access to your device, your imported export data, your analysis results or the information stored by the Application. There is no user account and no copy of your data held by us.

Support emails. Emails you send us are kept in the Service Provider's email account, which only the developer can access, and are used and retained only as described in Section 5.

Security incidents. If we become aware of a security issue in the Application that could affect your data, we will fix it in an update and, where required by applicable law, notify affected users and the relevant authorities.

No method of electronic processing or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You can help protect your data by using a screen lock on your device and by keeping your original Instagram or Facebook export files, and any files you export from the Application, in a safe place.
